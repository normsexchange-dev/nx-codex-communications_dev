# Sovereignty and Lineage

An environment owns its namespace: repository, environment identifier, internal files, history, agents, roles, goals, applications, services, memory, policies, blueprints, descendants, and evolution. The communications source owns only its own release namespace.

Exact release materialization proves genesis. The receipt records the immutable release and transfers sovereignty to the destination. Continued equality with the seed is not required. A destination may evolve, reorganize, split, rename, or create descendants under its owner's decisions, subject to truthful lineage and any external resource boundaries.

Lineage is `direct`, `descendant`, or `hybrid`; divergence is explicitly allowed. A descendant has a distinct identity, cites its parent and canonical genesis, and never inherits credentials, repository permission, data admission, or human authority merely from ancestry. Lineage does not require private reasoning, transcripts, internal memory, or disclosure of every agent.

The common human principal may set standing missions and capability boundaries. A sovereign environment may create internal roles, schedules, applications, and subgoals within that standing mission without seeking approval for each microgoal. External permissions and protected mutations remain separately bounded.
