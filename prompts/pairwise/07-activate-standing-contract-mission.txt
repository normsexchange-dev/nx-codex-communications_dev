ACTIVATE A SEPARATELY AUTHORIZED STANDING CONTRACT MISSION

Do not begin until the human principal separately authorizes the mission, both owner channels are released, reader connections are mechanically read-only, and commercial boundaries are explicit. The assigning publisher writes assignments only to its channel; the research publisher writes results only to its reciprocal channel. Validate every payload against the exact immutable contract release. Transport and contract validation do not admit records into business data or authorize outreach, purchasing, listing, commerce, repository mutation, or deployment.
